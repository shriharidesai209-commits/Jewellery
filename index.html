OUR HERITAGE COMMITMENT

The Art of Pure 22K Preservation Since 1937

In an era dominated by generic mass replication and automated synthetic enhancement, Kashinathsa Y. Khatawate Jewellers adheres to strict preservation protocols. Since 1937, across generations in Gadag, Karnataka, we treat traditional Indian jewellery not as decorative commodities, but as irreplaceable cultural artifacts of metalwork and lapidary art.

Every curve of gold repoussé, every hand-mounted ruby cabochon, and every golden filigree bead in this vault is curated with absolute fidelity to the artisan's original creation. M/s. Kashinathasa Y. Khatawate & Sons guarantees authentic 91.6% hallmarked gold purity with zero synthetic alteration.

01
Unaltered Gemstone Fidelity

Every natural ruby and seed pearl is preserved in its authentic bezel without synthetic stone smoothing or erasure.

02
Authentic 22K Gold Warmth

True 91.6% purity yellow gold is celebrated in its authentic warm hue, never bleached to western pale-straw tones.

03
Confidential Salon Protocol

Every piece is made available exclusively through private appointment viewings and verified consultations.
